What City Legacy Agents do

Agents operate under Master Admin authority. They onboard customers within assigned limits, fund accounts from their licensed float, and support only the clients in their portfolio.

Customer onboarding

Create verified customer profiles. Account and routing numbers are issued automatically by the bank system — never typed by hand.

Responsible funding

Move funds only from your agent float to customers you created. Float is provisioned and audited by Master Admin.

Care & controls

Freeze or restrict your own customers when risk appears. Message them securely. Master Admin retains full override power.

Standards you uphold

Every agent represents the City Legacy name. That means clear identity, clean records, and respect for customer limits set by Master Admin.

  • ✓Licensed and authorized access only — credentials are issued by Master Admin.
  • ✓Customer limits protect the network; expansions require Master Admin approval.
  • ✓Color-coded portfolios in Admin keep every agent’s book of business distinct.
  • ✓Mail and activity for your customers stay in your portal; Master Admin sees everything.
